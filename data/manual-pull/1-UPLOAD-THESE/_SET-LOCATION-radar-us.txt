HOW THESE WERE CHOSEN — and what bias is left.
Generated mechanically from teamz-company-automation/data/rpm-benchmarks.json (Ezoic 2025 +
Mediavine EPMV, updated 2026-04-13), ranked by measured US CPM, highest first. The lane ORDER is
the table's, not anyone's judgement. Regenerate and the same order comes out.

Two exclusions, both stated so they can be argued with:
  - b2b-leadgen ($50-300) tops the table but the file's own units note says it is priced PER LEAD,
    not per 1000 impressions. It is a different business model and cannot be compared to CPM lanes
    in an ads play. It is NOT excluded on preference — if the goal ever shifts from ads to lead
    generation, it should be the FIRST thing tested.
  - Lanes under $10 CPM (technology $7, productivity $6.5, fitness $7, lifestyle $5.5, creative
    $4, gaming $3.5, entertainment $2.5, memes $1.2). The cut is a number, not a taste — and this
    site already proves the point: its entire existing footprint sits in technology/productivity
    and Planner returned 50/mo for ~35 of 38 of its keywords.

Earlier versions of this file WERE biased and are recorded here so it does not happen again:
  v1  the site's own dead vocabulary (fileprovider, espresso test) — Planner said 50/mo.
  v2  a guess at "high-RPM niches": ten of sixteen seeds landed in the $7/$6.5 bottom tier while
      every lane above $20 was omitted.
  v3  8 of 26 lanes, hand-picked — and inconsistently: career-jobs ($16) and health-general ($13)
      were included while longevity ($25), business-saas ($22) and health-supplements ($20) were
      dropped. Higher-paying lanes cut, lower-paying lanes kept. Indefensible.
  v4  (this one) every CPM lane >= $10, ordered by the table.

Seed PHRASING is still human: two 1-3 word entry points per lane. Short on purpose — P(AI Overview)
climbs 9.5% at 1 word to 46.4% at 7+ across 146M SERPs, and this site ranks position 4 for a 9-word
query and takes zero clicks because Google prints the answer inline. Seeds are doors, not targets:
Planner returns the real long tail behind each one with volume and competition attached.

EXPECT HEAVY REFUSALS. build-course-radar.py rejects vanity heads this site cannot win, clusters
dominated by one term, duplicates of existing courses, and AIO-dense clusters (median query >= 6
words). Most of these probably die there — that is the filter working, not failing.

VERDICT AGREED IN ADVANCE, so neither side moves the goalposts afterwards:
  >= 3 clusters survive  -> a real path; the engine starts building courses on its own.
  0-2 clusters survive   -> ads cannot reach $1k/mo on this site; switch to a services funnel.
