WHY THIS CHANGED (2026-07-19):
The previous batch asked Keyword Planner for the volume of terms this site ALREADY targets
(fileprovider, espresso test, android storage...). Those came back at 50/mo for ~35 of 38
terms — Planner's bottom bucket, i.e. no measurable demand. Asking for the volume of
keywords we already rank for can only confirm what we already have; it can never find the
demand we are missing. Discovery can.

STEPS (Google Ads > Tools > Keyword Planner):
  1. Click "Discover new keywords"   (NOT "Get search volume and forecasts")
  2. Tab "Start with keywords" — paste every line of batch-radar-us-01.csv
  3. LOCATIONS = United States       <- wrong location = wrong volume = wasted pull
  4. Language = English
  5. Download the results CSV
  6. Save it into: data/manual-pull/2-DROP-RESULTS-HERE/

The 16 seeds deliberately span six niches (office/data tools, dev, cloud certs, AI,
marketing, finance, interview prep). They are SEEDS, not targets — Planner returns hundreds
of related keywords with real volume and competition. build-course-radar.py then scores
every returned keyword by expected $/month = RPM(niche, country) x winnable volume, and
picks the course topic itself. No niche is pre-chosen here; the money decides.

The export header may read "Keyword (by relevance)" instead of "Keyword" — the parser
handles both as of 2026-07-19.
